## Question

Whether the poorer target-plane field is caused mainly by amplitude modulation, phase-board exit-field measurement error, or focal-plane displacement.

Earlier full-flow results showed:

- design/best field distance: `16.00 / 18.79 mm`;
- shift: about `2.79 mm`;

## Interpretation

The `16 mm` value is the intended propagation/design distance. It is not the same as the phase-board exit diagnostic plane. The exit diagnostic plane is near the physical board exit / short probe distance, while the focus search evaluates target-field quality along z.

## Reporting Rule

- design distance;
- best z distance;
